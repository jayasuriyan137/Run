YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

Data & privacy

A public app with a journal that stays in your browser. No account or cloud sync.

Export your workouts, reviews, splits, training check-ins, session notes, goals and food journal.

How your data is stored

  • New visitors start with an empty journal.
  • Entries and imported backups stay in this browser's local storage.
  • No analytics, application API calls or uploads are made.
  • Data does not sync between browsers, devices or site addresses.
  • Export a backup before clearing browser storage. Backups can contain health information.
  • The public source contains no personal workout history.
